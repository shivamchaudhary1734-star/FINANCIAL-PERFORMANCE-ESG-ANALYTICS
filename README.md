  FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE & ESG ANALYTICS 

  Financial Performance | Revenue Growth | Profitability | ESG & Sustainability

  Tools & Technologies
  Python: Data cleaning, data analysis, and exploratory data analysis
  SQL: Business queries, aggregations, and company comparisons
  Power BI: Interactive financial and ESG dashboard

  Dataset

The dataset includes company information, industry, region, year, revenue, profit margin, market capitalization, growth rate, ESG scores, carbon emissions, water usage, and energy consumption.

Business Questions
Which companies generate the highest revenue?
Which industries have the best average profit margins?
How does revenue change year over year?
Which regions generate the highest revenue?
Which companies have the highest market capitalization?
Which companies have the highest ESG scores?
Which companies generate the highest carbon emissions?
How does financial performance compare with ESG performance?
Which companies perform above their industry’s average profit margin?
Which companies combine strong financial performance with high ESG scores?

Project Workflow
Data inspection and cleaning using Python.
Financial and ESG analysis using SQL.
KPI development and dashboard creation in Power BI.
Business insights and performance comparisons.

Key Metrics
Total Revenue
Average Profit Margin
Market Capitalization
Revenue Growth
Average ESG Score
Carbon Emissions

Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents financial performance, profitability, revenue trends, and ESG indicators.

Conclusion

The project demonstrates how Python, SQL, and Power BI can be used together to analyze financial performance and sustainability indicators and support data-driven business decisions.
